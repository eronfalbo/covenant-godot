# Covenant — Design Bible

Salvaged from Notion (Feb 2026). The Godot game is now the source of truth for
mechanics. This file preserves the IP: theology, characters, language, and narrative
vision that inform event writing and future development.

---

# Part 1: Core Thesis

Victory is transmission, not conquest. The player IS the covenant consciousness — the
living thread of wisdom persisting across vessels, generations, and civilizations.

Human history is a dance between two models of organizing civilization:
- **Adon model**: recognition, covenant, portable knowledge, living chain
- **Bel model**: ownership, administration, fixed archive, centralized control

Neither is purely evil. Both respond to real human needs. But only one preserves freedom.

# Part 2: The Adon and the Bel

## Adon (אדון) — The One Recognized
The root implies sovereignty, authority. But Adon does not mean owner. An Adon is
recognized, acknowledged, honored. The relationship is granted, not imposed. You can
leave an Adon. Recognition can be withdrawn.

This is why YHVH is called Adonai — "my Lord, the one I recognize" — but never Baal.

In the game world, settlements acknowledge their guardian angel as Adon. The chain holds:
You → Adon (angel) → El Elyon (God Most High).

## Bel/Baal (בעל) — The One Who Owns
The root means to own, to possess. A baal habayit is a homeowner. When a city has a god,
that god is the Baal of that city. You cannot leave a Baal. Belonging is not a choice.

Bel = Akkadian/Mesopotamian form. Baal = Canaanite form. Nimrod names his city Ba-Bel —
"In the Bel."

## How Adon Becomes Bel (three moves)
1. **Enslavement of Labor.** Ham binds Bnei Elohim to one city. The angel becomes "their"
   angel — an owner, not a presence they commune with as they pass through.
2. **Theological Promotion.** The Adon is declared divine in itself, not a messenger of
   the One God. The chain connecting local to Source is severed.
3. **Political Legitimation.** Whoever controls the shrine controls divine legitimacy.
   "The Bel chose me to lead. The Bel demands your labor."

None of these moves is obviously evil at any single step. Step 1 looks like good
management. Step 2 looks like local religious expression. Step 3 looks like political
stability. You must see the whole arc to understand the trap.

## The Prophetic Vision: Ishi, Not Baali (Hosea 2:16)
The messianic endgame: not just defeating the Bel system, but transforming the Adon
relationship into something even more intimate — Ish and Ishah, partner, mutual covenant.

# Part 3: Bnei Adam and Bnei Elohim

## The Aleph-Dam Foundation
Adam (אדם) = Aleph (א, divine breath) + Dam (דם, blood/biological life).
Before the fall: aleph perfectly integrated with dam. After the fall: the aleph separates.
The fruit introduces the gap between knower and known. Now there is an "I" that looks
AT God rather than WITH God.

## Bnei Elohim — Children of God
The original model. Never ate the fruit. Never experienced the fall into self-consciousness.

Superior in every way the garden-state was superior:
- Physically integrated — no internal conflict
- Cognitively unified — they don't analyze nature, they participate in it
- In communion with the natural world — the we-mode is the God-mode
- Called "sons of God" because they haven't separated from divine unity

In the game: scattered across the Near East, living in bands. Oral culture, tool use, art.
Not primitive — pre-covenantal. Terrified flood survivors hiding in highlands.

## Bnei Adam — Children of the Conscious One
Those who carry fruit-consciousness. They suffer. They choose. They covenant.
What they have that Bnei Elohim lack: the capacity for covenant. Covenant requires choice.
Choice requires self-consciousness. Self-consciousness requires the separation of aleph
from dam.

## The Two Creations (Genesis 1 vs Genesis 2)
Genesis 1 = creation of humanity as species, still in divine image.
Genesis 2 = specific formation of covenantal Adam who receives the commandment.
Bnei Elohim are Genesis 1 humanity. Bnei Adam are Genesis 2 and descendants.

## Genesis 6: The Catastrophe
"The sons of God saw the daughters of man, that they were beautiful, and they took them
as wives." (Gen 6:2)

Bnei Elohim's superior capacities entered the Adamic world without covenant, without
initiation, without the chain. Result: Nephilim — consciousness without covenant, power
without restraint.

The "forbidden arts" (metallurgy, weapons, cosmetics, astrology) — not knowledge from
heaven, but garden-state knowledge retained from 100k years close to nature, now detached
from its source. Technique without communion. Technology without wisdom.

## The Post-Flood Inversion
Pre-flood: garden overpowered covenant (Bnei Elohim → Bnot Adam = Nephilim).
Post-flood: covenant (corrupted) captures garden (Ham → Bnei Elohim = contented subjects).

Ham keeps Bnei Elohim in we-mode on purpose. Redirects their natural communion toward
the Bel of his city. The communion FEELS the same from inside. The bondage is perfect
because it's undetectable.

## Initiation
Bnei Elohim can be brought into covenant through proper chain transmission. Painful
transition — they lose garden-state communion and gain ego-consciousness. Some don't
survive. The fall is real and dangerous.

If mixing happens without initiation — Nephilim problem returns.

# Part 4: Characters

Every character has a legitimate motivation. No cartoonish villains.

## The Family (Tutorial Period)

**Noah** — Last man who walked with God before the Flood. Carries the complete chain.
Authority absolute at start but fades as new generations grow who didn't witness the Flood.
Must transmit, not impose. Speaks in short sentences, metaphors, and memory.

**Naamah** (Noah's wife) — Keeper of pre-Flood knowledge. The women's strand of the
chain — patterns, remedies, lullabies. Close to Canaan, compensating for the curse.
Teaches him secretly. Sees what Noah misses. First to stand after mourning.

**Shem** — The chain carrier. Patient, precise, cautious. Wants to GO — fill the earth,
carry the covenant outward. Seed of Abraham. Becomes player vessel after Noah.
Voice: precise and cautious, ritual-minded.
- YES: "Permanence is not the danger. Concentration is."
- NO: "The key risk factor is centralization of resources."

**Ham** (חם = warmth) — The producer. The builder. The one who feeds everyone. NOT the
villain. His deepest motivation: fear. He witnessed the Flood. He never wants to be that
vulnerable again. So he builds in stone, centralizes power. His solution is wrong but not
stupid. Voice: practical, warm, solution-oriented.
- YES: "Let me quarry stone. Let me make something that lasts."
- NO: "I propose we invest in permanent infrastructure."

**Japheth** — The guardian. The boundary-keeper. Virtue is loyalty. Direct, observant,
tactical. Agrees with Shem about Shinar because two rivers = natural walls, defensible.
Voice: direct, minimal, data-driven.
- YES: "How many fires burned last year?"
- NO: "What was the festival attendance rate?"

**Canaan** — Ham's firstborn. Conceived on the ark when Ham broke containment (Sanhedrin
108b). Born right after the diagnosis ritual (Y0 S3). The curse is not punishment — it is
diagnosis. Naamah is close to him. As he grows, where Ham acts from love, Canaan acts
from something sharper.

## Post-Tutorial Characters (future)

**Tamar** — Senior Ben Chanoch. Ground-truth voice. Reports what's actually happening.
**Ashur** — Scout who discovers Bnei Elohim (post-tutorial full discovery event).
**Kenan** — Young man who leaves for Ham's settlement. First personal drifter.
**Yoktan** — Ham's eldest in southern settlement. Face of efficiency without wisdom.
**Mered** — Bnei Elohim man absorbed by Ham's city. The Enkidu figure. Proto-Nephilim.
**Nimrod** — Not the warrior-king himself but the handler/ideologue. Hammurabi = חם הוא רבי.
**Eber** — "The one who crosses." His name becomes Hebrew. Guards the Language of Angels.

## Population Categories (post-tutorial)

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Bnei Adam (Covenant) | Shem's people. Carry the chain. I-mode. |
| Bnei Chanoch | Trained seers. Can perceive Adon vs Baal, initiate, teach. |
| Bnei Elohim | Pre-covenantal. Garden-state. Superior but without covenant. |
| Hamites | Ham's Bnei Adam. City-builders. Drifted but not corrupted. |
| Canaanites | Canaan's line. Fusion approach (Byblos). Become Phoenicians. |
| Yephetites | Japheth's people. The swing population. |
| Communers | Captured Bnei Elohim in city-Bel worship. Appear happy. |
| Drifters | Bnei Adam who lost chain connection. Recoverable. |

# Part 5: Language Guide

The voice of Covenant is primal, tactile, and biblical. Characters who live in tents and
work with their hands do not speak in abstractions.

**The Rule:** If a word would sound natural in a boardroom, hospital, or university
lecture, it does not belong. If it sounds natural spoken around a fire by a man with
calloused hands, it stays.

## Forbidden → Replacement

**Bureaucratic:**
- assigned → sent, given work, spoken for
- available → idle, unspoken-for, free
- resources → stores, grain, livestock, what we have
- community → camp, tents, people
- standard → fitting, due, proper

**Modern/Abstract:**
- focus on → put them to, send them to
- options → paths, ways
- efficient → it works
- self-sufficient → we have enough of our own
- consolidating → one hand grips all

**Institutional:**
- training → teaching, raising
- student → young one, disciple
- security → watchmen
- successor → the one who follows him

**Financial:**
- afford → have enough for
- invest → give, spend, pour out
- diplomacy → good will, trust between brothers
- transmission → the teaching, passing the fire

**Scientific:**
- astronomical knowledge → knowing the stars
- calendar precision → when the seasons turn
- navigation → which way to walk
- scouts → men walking the far country

## UI Language
- "Where shall they serve:" not "Available assignments:"
- "The year turns" not "Year-end administration"
- "See the state of things" not "View detailed status"
- FIRM / CRACKING not OK / NEED WORK
- BURNING / FLICKERING not OK / LOW

## The Test
Read every line aloud. If you can hear it spoken by a man standing at an altar on a
mountain near Ararat, with smoke rising behind him, it passes. If it sounds like it
belongs in a spreadsheet, a memo, or a textbook, rewrite it.

# Part 6: The Canaan Cascade (Post-Tutorial Arc)

The chain of cause-and-effect that drives the post-tutorial game:

1. **Canaan settles in Shem's land, builds Sidon.** First boundary violation.
2. **Shem tolerates Canaan.** The correct response is patience. Canaan's children absorb
   the alphabet through proximity. Phoenicians carry 22 letters across Mediterranean.
   "Servant of servants" fulfilled: Canaan serves by carrying.
3. **Nimrod seizes Shinar.** Builds Erech, Akkad, Calneh. The commons becomes empire.
4. **Salem conceived as response.** A city that IS covenant, not administration.
5. **Nimrod builds Babel as counter-Salem.** Salem unifies by transcendence. Babel by absorption.
6. **The Convergence.** Akkad + Egypt → two Bels becoming one Bel.
7. **God scatters.** 4.2 kiloyear drought. 70 suppressed visions of centrality burst free.

# Part 7: Chain Integrity Vision (Post-Tutorial)

The full game's Living Fire system (currently simplified for tutorial):

## Visibility Tiers
| Chain | Tier | Player Experience |
|-------|------|-------------------|
| 80-100 | White Heat | See the whole board. Proactive decisions. |
| 50-79 | Healthy | See nearby. Must invest in scouting. |
| 25-49 | Danger Zone | Blind. Crises ambush you. Firefighting every turn. |
| 0-24 | Critical | Near-total blindness. Chain almost dead. |

## The Essene Ending (Chain = 0)
> The last Ben Chanoch died in the winter. His student could recite every word of every
> teaching, but when he spoke them, nothing moved. The words were correct. The fire was gone.
>
> In the generations that followed, they preserved everything. They copied the scrolls.
> They maintained the calendar. They observed the festivals with perfect precision.
>
> But no one could remember what the festivals meant.
>
> The Dead Sea kept the scrolls. The people forgot.

## Day of Immersion Exception
Once per year, regardless of Chain tier, the player sees EVERYTHING. Full map overlay,
exact numbers, exact faction scores. If the fast is ended early: reduced data. The
spiritual shortcut costs strategic intelligence.

# Part 8: Root Degradation Flavour

What each root looks like when it's healthy vs. dying:

| Root | Healthy | Degrading |
|------|---------|-----------|
| **Star Watch** (Cosmic) | Young men climb nightly. Calendar precise. Festivals announced months ahead. | Nobody climbs. Southern camps can't see the hilltop. Dates guessed at. |
| **Altar Road** (Spatial) | Clear paths to altar. Pilgrims travel safely. | Road overgrown. Families miss festivals — can't find the way. |
| **Ritual Order** (Procedural) | Teva performs in exact sequence. Every word, gesture, timing. | Substitute performs incorrectly. Nobody notices except Shem. |
| **Living Tongue** (Embodied) | The body remembers — movements, gestures, chanting while walking. | People read the words but can't feel the rhythm. Dead marks replace living practice. |
| **Festival Calendar** (Temporal) | Three gatherings, everyone attends, timing exact. | Far camps hold their own observances — close but not quite right. |
| **Sacred Objects** (Material) | Altar stones maintained. Ark timbers preserved. | Families burn Ark timbers for firewood. "It's just old wood." |
| **Teaching Network** (Communal) | Every camp has a resident teacher. | Camps without teachers lose contact. Entire generation grows up knowing festivals but not reasons. |
| **The Stories** (Textual) | Creation, Flood, genealogies told identically everywhere. | Small changes accumulate. Flood lasted "forty days." It lasted a year. |

# Part 9: The Five Movements (Post-Tutorial Narrative)

## Movement I: The Ark and the Republic (Years 0-35)
Warm, hopeful. Tutorial + early expansion. Bnei Elohim discovery is the inciting incident.

## Movement II: The Awakening (Years 35-70)
Wonder + tension. Bnei Elohim responding to initiation. Ham's different method visible.
Core asymmetry: covenant takes years per person; city absorbs hundreds overnight.

## Movement III: The Fracture (Years 70-120)
Grief, urgency. Republic is over. Cuneiform question. Noah confronts Ham. Ruins
discovered. Japheth wavers. The defining emotion: watching people you love make choices
you cannot stop.

## Movement IV: The Tower and the Influence (Years 120-180)
Awe and dread. Ham's city rises. It's beautiful and it works. Salem conceived as the
answer — not a competing city, but a demonstration that the city model can work under
covenant.

## Movement V: The Council (Years 180-200)
Elegy, resolution. Noah aging. The fire passes to Shem. The 70 fires at the final
Festival of Shelters are the visual verdict. The transition feels not like death but
like graduation.

# Part 10: The Covenant Calendar

## Seasons and Festivals
SEASONS = ["Autumn", "Winter", "Spring", "Summer"]

| Festival | Season | Purpose |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Festival of Shelters | Autumn | 7-day ingathering. The 70 Fires. |
| Day of Immersion | Autumn (10th Moon 7) | Annual clarity. Full visibility override. |
| Festival of Adam's Gratitude | Winter | 8 nights of fire. Gift exchange. Redistribution. |
| Mourning of Abel | Summer | Fast. Annual check on violence and justice. |
| Festival of the Covenant | Spring | Pilgrimage. Seven Words proclaimed. |

**Design rule:** Use the game's own calendar. NO Hebrew/Babylonian month names.
NO "Marcheshvan." Moon of Shelters, Moon of Fire, etc.
